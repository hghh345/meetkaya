FILE_001 THE MIND / how I think +
INTERNAL PROCESSES / ACTIVE

My brain doesn't work in straight lines. I'm a pattern-seeker, an investigator and a synthesiser. I'm interested in why things work, why they don't, and what might be hiding underneath the obvious explanation.

How I approach a problem

  • Observe. Gather information, context and perspectives.
  • Investigate. Ask questions and look beneath the surface.
  • Connect. Find relationships, patterns and contradictions.
  • Define. Identify the central insight and what it means.
  • Translate. Turn the thinking into strategy and creative work.

I don't believe strategy should live in a presentation that nobody opens again. The thinking should make its way into the actual work.

FILE_002 THE INVESTIGATION / what I look for +
RESEARCH / DISCOVERY / SYNTHESIS

Before deciding what a brand should say, I want to understand what is really happening.

Questions I tend to ask

  • What is the real problem beneath the stated problem?
  • What does the audience actually care about?
  • What makes this business different, beyond what it claims?
  • What is being overlooked or misunderstood?
  • What story would make all these pieces make sense?

I bring together research, audience understanding, brand context and creative thinking to find a useful point of view.
