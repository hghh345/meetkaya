03. WHAT I ACTUALLY DO

I work in the space between the big idea and the thing people actually experience. That might mean figuring out what a brand should stand for, finding the story nobody has spotted yet, or building the creative systems that bring it all to life. Usually, it's a bit of everything.

04. A LITTLE ABOUT THE HUMAN

I'm Kaya, a brand architect working at the intersection of narrative, culture, systems and experiential design.

My background spans immersive events, interactive web experiences, brand storytelling, community-led marketing and creative direction with a strong focus on building emotionally resonant brands that people don't just consume, but participate in.

I've led projects across fashion, CPG, experiential nightlife, digital media and startup environments, often owning initiatives end-to-end: from concept development and audience positioning to creative production, cross-functional coordination, launch strategy and operational execution.

What excites me most is helping brands scale without losing their distinct voice, creating the frameworks, experiences and storytelling systems that make brands feel culturally alive rather than corporate.

I'm not particularly interested in being different for the sake of it. I'm interested in finding the difference that actually matters and making something real out of it.
