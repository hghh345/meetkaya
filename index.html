01 / THE CENTRAL QUESTION

OBSERVATION_001

What if the most interesting thing about your business is the thing you haven't figured out how to explain yet?

Maybe the positioning is too familiar. Maybe the story is underselling the ambition. Maybe there's an entirely different way to think about the business that nobody has explored.

I'm interested in the space between what a company is, what it means to people, and what it has the potential to become. That's where I like to work.

02 / HOW MY BRAIN WORKS

FOUR CONNECTED FORCES

I look beneath the surface before I decide what to build on it.

My process isn't a straight line from brief to deliverable. It's a way of looking at things: noticing what others miss, recognising what has potential, imagining what isn't there yet, and giving those ideas a structure that can exist in the world.

01

Perception

Seeing what others miss. Finding patterns, asking better questions, making unexpected connections and getting underneath the problem rather than immediately accepting how it's been framed.

02

Discernment

Knowing what makes something interesting. Recognising the difference between what's distinctive and what's simply different, what's culturally relevant and what's just another passing trend.

03

Imagination

Seeing what something could become. Connecting ideas, developing narratives, creating creative territories and imagining worlds that give a business a recognisable point of view.

04

Architecture

Turning possibility into something tangible. Building the strategy, story, frameworks and creative expressions that connect an idea to the people it's meant to reach.

03 / THE WAY I APPROACH THINGS

FROM POSSIBILITY TO REALITY

I don't just want to work with the brief. I want to question what the brief could be.

01 / LOOK CLOSER

Find the real problem.

Investigate the business, its audience, its category and its cultural context. Look for the gap between what exists and what people could understand, feel or experience.

02 / FIND THE THREAD

Discover what makes it matter.

Identify the distinctive idea, tension, perspective or opportunity that can give the business a more meaningful and memorable place in people's minds.

03 / IMAGINE THE WORLD

Build a more interesting direction.

Develop the narrative, positioning, creative territory and personality that make the idea recognisable, compelling and worth paying attention to.

04 / MAKE IT REAL

Give the idea somewhere to go.

Translate the thinking into strategy, messaging, campaigns, content, digital experiences and the practical tools that help a company move forward.

04 / WHERE THE THINKING GOES

AREAS OF PRACTICE

The work can take different forms. The thread connecting it is making an idea more meaningful, distinctive and real.

01

Strategic Architecture

Research, positioning, audience insight, brand strategy, differentiation and the frameworks that give a business a clearer direction.

02

Narrative Architecture

Brand storytelling, messaging systems, founder narratives, verbal identity and the stories that help people understand why a company exists and why it matters.

03

World-Building & Creative Direction

Creative territories, brand personality, conceptual development and the visual and verbal ideas that create a recognisable world around a business.

04

Real-World Expression

Campaigns, websites, content strategy, editorial systems, pitch decks and digital experiences that bring the bigger idea into everyday contact with its audience.

05 / THE HUMAN BEHIND THE HYPERACTIVE BRAIN

SUBJECT: KAYA

SUBJECT PROFILE / CREATIVE THINKER

KNOWN SIDE EFFECTS: TOO MANY TABS OPEN

Part strategist. Part storyteller. Part professional question-asker.

My background spans brand strategy, storytelling, content, campaigns, audience growth and digital execution. I've worked with startups, founder-led businesses and established brands to shape how they communicate and connect with people.

Over the years, I've moved between research and creative development, big-picture thinking and the details that make an idea work. I enjoy getting close to a business, understanding what makes it tick, and finding the story or opportunity that hasn't been fully expressed yet.

I'm especially interested in ambitious teams building something with a point of view — people who care about how their work is understood by the world, and who are willing to explore a direction that isn't immediately obvious.

I'm not interested in making unconventional ideas more conventional. I'm interested in discovering the logic, opportunity and potential inside them — and building something extraordinary around it.
