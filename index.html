one thing i believe

A good brand doesn't just tell people what something is. It helps them imagine what could be possible.

01 / make

I like making things that work.

From brand strategy and identity to websites, campaigns, print and physical experiences. I like being close enough to the work that an idea doesn't get lost between the brief and the finished thing.

02 / think

I get curious before I get visual.

What's the actual problem? Who has it? What do they believe already? What needs to change? I want to understand the product, customer and category before deciding what the brand should look like.

03 / build

Sometimes the designer is also the developer.

I have shipped websites myself, worked inside the stack, prototyped with new tools, and used AI as part of the making process—not as a substitute for taste or thinking.

04 / play

I make a lot of things nobody asked me to make.

This has turned out to be useful. Side projects keep me curious, help me learn new tools, and make it harder for me to believe there's only one way to do something.

05 / radial

The science is moving faster than the story.

That's what grabbed me about Radial. There is a fascinating brand problem hiding inside the clinical one: how do you make someone believe there might be another possibility without asking them to believe blindly?

I don't know the answer yet. I'd like to find it with you.
