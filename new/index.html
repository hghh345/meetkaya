i find the story.

inside her brain / entry 002
02 / how i think

my brain doesn't work in straight lines.

i'm a pattern-seeker, an investigator and a natural synthesiser.

i'm interested in why things work, why they don't, and what might be hiding underneath the obvious explanation.

i like to get inside a problem before jumping to a solution. i look at the context, the people involved, the psychology behind their decisions, and the connections between seemingly unrelated things.

my process, more or less:

  • observe — collect information and ask questions
  • connect — look for patterns and contradictions
  • understand — identify the real problem
  • imagine — explore what could be possible
  • build — translate the thinking into something tangible

i don't believe strategy should live in a presentation that nobody opens again.
